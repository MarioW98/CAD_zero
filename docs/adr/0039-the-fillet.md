## Context — why the fillet is the first act that does not take away

removes material, the boundary keeps its pieces, the counts shrink by

rounded edge is not a boolean: it is the ball that rolls, the envelope
surface it sweeps, the two adjacent faces that RETREAT to the tangent
curves, the edge that dies and the flank that is born in its place.

  carried — the shrunk adjacent faces keep their native frames and

#### 1. LO SPIGOLO D'ANGOLO DEL BOX (plane×plane) — the cylindrical flank

edge name, the naming layer's own vocabulary, resolvable before any

*The counts, derived step by step (the walk visible).* The edge dies

the two adjacent faces and the two end faces RETREAT (absorbed: the
