## Decision

### D1. The five metrics — each with its definition, its tool, its discriminating proof, and its antidote

Every metric has four parts, always, and a metric missing any of the
four is not registered: the **definition** (what is measured, in terms a
test can fail), the **tool** (what measures it — a suite member, a
corpus, a protocol, an oracle), the **discriminating proof** (the test
that fails on the naive version — the ADR-0034 D4 / ADR-0035 D5 /
ADR-0036 D5 protocol, verbatim: every claim has the test that fails on
the naive answer, agreed before the code), and the **antidote** (the
slogan the metric exists to kill — because a metric that cannot name
the lie it prevents is a vanity number, and vanity numbers are how
feature counting came back dressed as quality).

#### D1.1 Metric 1 — the family census: a family is supported when its row is whole

**Definition.** A geometric family is **correctly supported** if and
only if its census row is complete — every column PASS, each column a
contract the CI testifies:

| Column | The contract |
|---|---|
| **writer** | a face can be BORN on the family: the authored surface class in the writer's form (the `CylinderZSurface`/`TorusZSurface` precedent — the class the first consumer lands, with its domain, its seam, its periodicity declared) |
| **frame** | the parametrization's frame rule — right-handed, ∂u×∂v the outward, **verified pointwise** over the declared windows (the derivation script's check, pinned) |
| **chirality** | the discriminating pin that guards the frame forever: the regression guard that FAILS on the left-handed authoring, plus the surviving evaluate pins (the m3 pattern, on the family's own surfaces) |
| **mass** | the mass family: volume, centroid, moments on (u,v) windows — **closed forms, or a declared quadrature with its declared tolerance** (never a hand-typed table); `all_exact` honest |
| **SSI rows** | the family's rows in the analytic SSI table with their dispositions: in-table typed, consumed at tangent limits by construction, or the WALL with the pair named — the register's `ssi_query` answers for the family before any boolean consumes it |
| **degenerates** | the family's degenerate dispositions PINNED: tangent, coincident, empty, near-band — each with its name, its band case's disposition, and its remedy (D1.2's zoo is this column's proof) |
| **serialization** | the round-trip: the family's parameters ride named fields, the golden's digests bit-identical N=10, the version doors honest at the bump |

A column that is not PASS is a DASH, and a dash is a registered debt
with a seat — "said, not hidden", the register's own law. **The register
counts a family as supported ONLY on a complete row**; a row with a
dash is progress, not support, and the register says so in the row.

**Tool.** The register's census table (the seat D6 declares; the rows
land with the probe's unit, backfilled for the families already in the
tree — the backfill itself the protocol's first paid audit). The
per-column evidence is the pinned test set the morsi already land; the
census does not invent new tests, it refuses to let existing tests be
silent about each other.

**Discriminating proof.** The census fails on the naive "feature
landed" count by construction: the cone counts as a feature (a class,
a flip, pins) and fails the census (no writer) — the difference between
the two counts IS the metric's discrimination, demonstrated on today's
tree without one new line of code.

**Antidote.** "N features landed." The feature count is dead as the
unit of account; the register's progress rows may still record morsi
(they are the history), but the STATUS question — what is supported —
is answered only by the census.

#### D1.2 Metric 2 — the pairwise degenerate zoo: robustness is a region, not a point

**Definition.** For every pair of families the exact path can compose
(boolean operands, fillet operands and their flanks), the degenerate
DISPOSITIONS are pinned in an adversarial corpus: internal and external
tangency, coincidence and co-radial cases, containment and empty
intersection, and the NEAR-BAND cases — the inputs constructed ON
PURPOSE at the band edges where the class changes (the sentinel's
citizens: just inside θ_strict, just outside, the straddling window,
the ties at the border resolved by the canonical key). Every band case
carries its PINNED DISPOSITION — which path answers, which tolerance is
declared, what the error says — never a disposition discovered at run
time.

**Tool.** The corpus as a suite member: the ADR-0039 D4 refusal
families' corpus (the fillet's registered, not-started tail) is the
first instance, generalized from the fillet to the pairs the census
admits. **Fuzz is the growth method**: parameter-space fuzzing over the
corpus's families and bands, deterministic by seed (ADR-0014's
discipline — the fuzz is reproducible or it is not evidence); every
crash the fuzz finds becomes a pinned row — the disposition decided,
the test landed, the register row recording the catch — never a silent
fix, the m6 lesson verbatim: a defect found by the zoo is a correction
with its own bite.

**Discriminating proof.** The zoo kills the per-case special algorithm
on contact — that is its purpose. An algorithm that answers the demo
path and guesses at the band edge produces a wrong disposition on some
zoo member by construction (the near-band member is BUILT to be the
case the demo never shows); the pinned disposition is the red the naive
answer must hit.

**Antidote.** "It works." The demo is one point in the input region;
the zoo is the region. Robustness claims are made against the corpus
or not made at all.

#### D1.3 Metric 3 — the chain benchmark: sequences, gated at every step, drift zero

**Definition.** An N-step operation sequence — the steps the workbench
can author, in the order a user can author them — executed with a GATE
after EVERY step, the gate checking the invariants the house already
derives per result, now checked as a standing contract on the
intermediate states:

1. every edge exactly **two opposite co-edges** (the radial structure,
   the census's own arithmetic — the count and the doubled count);
2. every loop **closed** (the walk returns; no open chain survives a
   step);
3. **χ even** — χ = V−E+F−H consistent with a closed orientable
   composition (the amended χ contract; for the sphere-topology body
   χ=2, the genus-1 additions 0 — the parity is the gate, the value the
   golden's);
4. the **census** — the tavola's closure both sides (kept + absorbed =
   the old generation; born + vanished = the new; the counts equal);
5. **drift = 0** — re-running the chain from the recipes (the DAG pass)
   reproduces every digest bit-identically (ADR-0014: the flags, the
   accumulation order, the quantizer — the chain re-run is the
   determinism clause's N-step form, the m4 N=10 round-trip grown a
   spine).

**Tool.** The chain test as a suite member, the digest oracle the H2
unit 6 pattern already carries (every face's full query answer as one
comparable string, the names riding the records). **Today's baseline is
chain-5: box → sketch → extrude → subtract → fillet** — the guide chain
the m6 protocol walked by hand (the box, the blind hole, the rim
filleted at r=0.25, the golden 14/20/9/H=1/χ=2, the volume
6.775059758517764, the radius edit silent through ONE undo). The
register pins chain-5 as today's number; longer chains and mid-chain
edits (D1.4's sequences feeding D1.3's gates) are the metric's growth
axis, each new chain a registered row with its N.

**Discriminating proof.** The chain gate fails on the single-op green:
a sequence can close every step's local contract and still break the
next step's input (the absorbed face the later reference needs; the
walk re-authored at step k unreadable at step k+2). The gate after
every step is what turns "each op is green" into "the sequence is
correct" — the m3 incident's granularity lesson at one remove: acts
that are each right can compose wrong, and only a standing gate sees
it.

**Antidote.** "Each operation is green." One boolean green proves one
boolean. Sequences are where parametric CAD actually lives and where
its topology actually breaks; the chain is the unit, not the step.

#### D1.4 Metric 4 — the reference survival: edits measured, deaths named

**Definition.** Against a feature-bearing chain (references held by
downstream features — the fillet's rim, the sketch's consumers), an
EDIT SEQUENCE runs: parameter edits in-band and out, re-authors that
split and replace, inserts that shift roles. The metric is the
**survival fraction** — references that resolve class 1
(`reference_resolved`, the feature re-running silently, ONE undo
entry) over the total — AND the accounting of every death: each death
carries its CLASS NAME and its payload, the four classes being
ADR-0036 D3's, verbatim, never re-derived here: MATCH
(`reference_resolved`), UNIQUE CONTINUER (`reference_renamed`, the
rename map declared, never silent), AMBIGUOUS
(`reference_ambiguous`, every candidate named, the reference does not
guess — "a reference is not a tolerance decision"), VANISHED
(`reference_vanished`, WHAT died, WHY — the tavola's vanished row
carries the cause — and the remedy). A death without its class name is
a crash; a survival number without the death ledger is a lie of
omission.

**Tool.** The resolver itself (landed, the four classes with machinery
and dispositions) and the D5 quartet as the test form: REGENERATION
(digests bit-identical), TOPOLOGICAL EDIT (the classes 3/4 with the
error that names), TOPOLOGY-PRESERVING EDIT (class 1, silent, no
reselection), the NAIVE COUNTERPROOF (the positional id fails where
the name holds). The metric's numbers are read from the quartet's
scenarios extended into sequences — the survival fraction and the
ledger reported per feature kind in the register's row.

**Discriminating proof.** The naive counterproof IS the
discrimination, already landed: the address-as-reference dies on the
edit the name survives. The metric extends it from one scenario to a
sequence ledger.

**Antidote.** "It is parametric." Parametric on the happy path is a
demo; parametric is a MEASURED survival under adversarial edits, every
exception an actionable error with a name. The claim without the
fraction is the slogan; the fraction without the ledger is the smaller
lie; the register carries both or neither.

#### D1.5 Metric 5 — the independent oracle: precision believed only when re-derived

**Definition.** A precision claim (a closed form, a volume, a moment,
a discriminator value) is VERIFIED only when an oracle that shares ZERO
computation code with the kernel re-derives it: an external CAS
checking the closed form symbolically; a declared-tolerance quadrature
or Monte Carlo checking the integrals numerically. The comparison road
may be the public query surface (the oracle must READ the kernel's
answer somehow); the DERIVATION road must be independent — different
method, different code, different failure modes. The m3 precedent is
the form: the toroidal wedge's closed form cross-checked in the tests
against a 1.6·10⁷-point quadrature, agreement to 2·10⁻⁶ relative — the
quadrature in the test, the recurrence in the kernel, the same
question asked twice by two roads. **The golden corpus is BY
CONSTRUCTION not independent** — its expected values were produced by
the machinery they judge (the classical formulas ARE the goldens
wherever a classical form exists, and that is exactly why they
qualify: the form pre-dates the kernel); a golden that is the kernel's
own output re-affirmed proves determinism, not truth. Independence is
the difference between verified and believed.

**Tool.** The oracle suite: a declared test member whose expected
values come from its own derivations (CAS where a form exists — pinned
by the derivation, reproducible; quadrature/Monte Carlo with the
tolerance DECLARED and the tolerance's own derivation honest about its
conditioning). Every census mass column's closed form and every
benchmark's baseline number carries its oracle's name.

**Discriminating proof.** The oracle fails on the self-affirming
golden: run the golden's expected value through the oracle's road and
a wrong form DISAGREES — the two roads converging on the right answer
is the only cross-check the house recognizes. (The m3 quadrature
already caught nothing — because the form was right; that is what a
passing oracle looks like. The metric exists for the day it catches
something.)

**Antidote.** "The golden passes." The golden proves the kernel agrees
with itself across runs and compilers — determinism, faithfully. Truth
needs the second road. The register will no longer record a precision
claim without its oracle's name beside it.

### D2. The family protocol — what a family declares, and who consumes it

**The protocol.** A family enters the exact world by DECLARING six
rows — the census columns of D1.1 made procedural, the frame and
its chirality pin declared as ONE row (the rule and its guard are
one act; the census audits them as two):

1. **the parametrization** — the writer's form: the surface class, its
   domain, its seam and periodicity, its placement knobs (the
   `CylinderZSurface`/`TorusZSurface` precedent: the class lands with
   the family's first consumer, in the consumer's axis, the registered
   classes keeping their own);
2. **the frame rule** — right-handed, ∂u×∂v outward, verified
   pointwise over every declared window, with the chirality pin that
   fails on the left-handed authoring (the ONE right-handed rule
   ADR-0032 planted; the flip's one-line form registered for any
   family found left-handed — the correction made ONCE, with the
   family's first writer, never cold);
3. **the mass** — closed forms for the (u,v) window integrals (the
   parts-recurrence family the cylindrical and toroidal patches
   already use — ONE recurrence closing every trig power the products
   reach), or a DECLARED quadrature with its tolerance where a form
   does not exist; `all_exact` says what is true;
4. **the band vocabulary** — which typed tolerance worlds the family
   reads (ADR-0035 D2's kinds — Linear, Parameter, the derived bands,
   Angular, Winding, Coplanar, Solve — zero bare constants), and the
   family's Jacobian declared so a linear tolerance may map into the
   parameter world as a NAMED derivation, per family, in the family's
   own terms — the D2.2 clause, now every family's obligation;
5. **the degenerate dispositions** — the zoo rows of D1.2 for every
   pair the family can compose, each disposition pinned before the
   corpus member lands;
6. **the serialization row** — the family's parameters as named fields
   (never a raw struct, never an address), the round-trip golden, the
   version-door arithmetic registered against the format's arbitration
   (never two bumps the same day).

**The consumers eat the protocol, not the class.** The boolean door
reads the family's declared SSI rows; the mass machinery reads the
declared forms (its out-of-class refusal retiring with each family's
landing — the torus's own precedent); the fillet reads the declared
bands and the declared dispositions; the panel reads the declared
parameters; the loader reads the declared fields. A consumer that
reaches past the protocol into a family's concrete class re-creates
the per-case coupling the instruction forbids — and the census is the
audit that catches it: a consumer's reach is visible as a census
column that cannot close for any OTHER family.

**The two legal axes of generalization — and the banned third.** (a)
**The protocol/data axis**: a new family lands by declaring the six
rows — new code, new tests, zero changes to the consumers; this is the
axis the census counts and the conquest measures (D3). (b) **The
algorithm axis**: option (ii) of ADR-0033 D2 — the declared
subdivision engine on the existing `Interval` type, the one honestly
approximated p-curve citizen behind the fence, priced as its own
delivery (unit 2.5), GATED by its registered entry criterion: "a real
case demanded by an author body … decided at the next internal
checkpoint, with the table and the fillet measured on the table —
empirical, never philosophical", with the maintainer's motivation
standing verbatim: «il muro è sempre onesto; la siepe, una volta
piantata, va sorvegliata per sempre; prima il muro, la siepe quando un
caso vero la domanda.» **The option (ii) gate is where an entry
criterion lives**: a family that cannot close its census by protocol
declaration and meets a real authored case does not get a special
algorithm — it fires the REGISTERED gate, and the gate's price (the
engine, the citizen, the eternal watch) is paid consciously or not at
all. The **banned** third axis is the per-case special algorithm: an
`if` tuned to one family's demo case, invisible to the census,
unpriceable at the gate. The instruction names it; this ADR makes it
unrepresentable: the register records no family the census cannot
see, and the census cannot see an algorithm that is not a declared
row.

**What the protocol is NOT.** It is not a promise that every family is
cheap — D3's baseline exists because entry costs REAL units. It is not
a generalization of the geometry: the vocabulary stays the analytic
one (conics in the domains, the pinned space-curve set), and a family
outside it (a general spline surface) is G3's question, refused by
name today exactly as ADR-0033 D2 refuses it. The protocol is the
CONTRACT SHAPE, not the capability ceiling — the ceiling stays the
wall's own.

Six units, one family, whole — and the register paid each in public,

### D4. The probe — the first family to enter after this ADR

The protocol's proof is not this document; it is the FIRST family that
enters under it. The probe is that family, and this ADR pins the
candidate set — **the ellipsoid, or the surface of revolution on a
general straight generatrix** (the line beyond the cylinder's parallel
seat and the cone's intersecting seat — the skew seat) — chosen at the
probe unit's own opening, from this set, by the review that opens it.
The reasons for the pin, and NOTHING else pre-written: both candidates
sit inside the analytic vocabulary the exact path already types (their
plane sections are conics; their SSI questions are the table's
questions); both stress the census differently (the ellipsoid: the

### D5. The benchmark suite — the numbers the register will carry

**The suite.** A declared benchmark set, run by the rite (both
compilers, the same presets the CI runs), its numbers recorded in the
register with the run's identity — the performance twin of the census:
capabilities get columns, costs get NUMBERS. The named members, none
of them new inventions (each an existing measured act, given a
standing home):

- **SDF evaluation** — batched vectorized evaluation throughput (the
  xsimd batch, serial by default — the ADR-0009 amendment's own
  numbers, now re-run per release);
- **marching cubes** — extraction time AND RAM peak (MC33 with vertex
  welding, the grid ladder's own measurements);
- **booleans** — the exact subtract/union on the guide cases, per
  body class;
- **fillet** — the two v1 classes' construction time, kernel-side (the
  m6 profile's own innocent numbers — set_fillet_radius 0.04 ms,
  rebuild_operations 0.11 ms — are the shape of the row);
- **feature-tree rebuild** — the DAG pass over the guide chain, per
  node count;
- **frame time** — the viewport's per-frame cost UNDER THE DECLARED
  BOUND (the m6-correttiva bound, verbatim: the frame's GL calls are
  O(the ranges drawn), NEVER O(the vertices));
- **import** — STL/OBJ load time on the corpus meshes;
- **picking** — the ray/AABB walk on the corpus scenes.

**The statistics.** Each member reports mean and p95 over a declared
repetition count, RAM peak where memory is the question, and the
thread count the run used (the parallel posture declared — oneTBB
off-by-default means the numbers are serial unless the row says
otherwise; a benchmark that silently changes posture is a lie with a
graph).

**The determinism clause.** Under the suite, every digest the tree
already pins (the goldens' round-trips, the chain re-runs, the
name/structure digests) is bit-identical — ADR-0014's clauses
(`-ffp-contract=off`, the fixed chunk-then-reduce order, the
quantizer) hold under measurement load, and no benchmark run may
re-seed, re-order or re-thread its way to a better number. **A
performance number bought with a determinism drift is not a number.**

**The declared-bound discipline.** Every performance claim is a
BOUND with its condition, never a "faster" — the MORSO C lesson,
made standing: the lag's cure was declared as an order (O(ranges),
never O(vertices)) and pinned by a test that counts the plumbing, not
a stopwatch alone. The suite's rows carry the bound first, the
measurement second. **The lag profile is the suite's first registered
item** — the m6-correttiva measurement (529,817 calls per frame →
O(the ranges drawn); 607.97 ms → 11.16 ms per frame; 1.64 → 89.62
fps; 84,080 → 125,552 triangles, the fillet now visible; the
per-vertex walk alone 338.24 ms with the GL calls mocked, the
GPU-independent discriminator) enters the register as the profile's
row zero: the form every future performance row takes — the cause
named, the bound declared, the before and after measured, the pins
holding it.

## Consequences

**Positive.** The unit of account becomes auditable: a family is
supported or it is a dash, a performance claim is a bound or it is
noise, a precision claim carries its oracle or it is belief. The
conquest gets a number (the torus baseline) and a direction (the
ratio falling). The next family's cost is estimable BEFORE it is
paid, in public, by the probe's derivation. The register's rows gain
the early-warning function the G2-u2 absence showed it lacked. The
per-case special algorithm becomes unrepresentable rather than merely
discouraged.

**Negative, said.** The census will show dashes where the register's
prose today reads as accomplishment — that is the point, and it will
hurt once, in the backfill. The metrics cost morsi: the zoo's corpus,
the chain gates, the oracle suite and the benchmark suite are real
units of work, each priced as its own delivery, none of them
geometry. The probe's ratio may NOT fall on the first entry — the
row must say so if it does not, and the protocol takes the
consequence (a second non-falling entry is the evidence the review
re-opens D2 with). The fuzz growth method commits the house to
reproducible seeds and pinned dispositions for every crash — the
zoo's ledger only grows.

**Neutral, registered.** The register's roadmap table gains the K
row — a phase whose scope is a counting regime rather than a
capability, the register's own unit of account turned into a row;
the cronaca stays untouched (history is not re-counted — the
old rows keep their units-landed voice; the census reads them, it
does not rewrite them).
